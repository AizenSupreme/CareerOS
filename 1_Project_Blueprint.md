# CareerOS — Project Blueprint 

## **Project title** 

AI-Powered Career Intelligence and Personalised Career Development System 

## **Problem statement** 

Students struggle to identify suitable careers, understand skill requirements, and follow a personalised plan toward job readiness. 

## **Target users** 

College students, fresh graduates, and early-career job seekers. 

## **Objectives** 

Recommend suitable careers; identify skill gaps; generate personalised learning roadmaps; track career readiness. 

## **Core features** 

Profile and assessment, career recommendation, skill-gap analysis, roadmap generation, readiness dashboard. 

## **Expected outcome** 

A working full-stack application that provides explainable career recommendations and actionable learning plans.
